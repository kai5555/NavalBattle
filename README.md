# NavalBattle

A small naval action game built in **Unity (C#)**, inspired by the sailing gameplay of *Assassin's Creed IV: Black Flag*.

## Gameplay

You captain a smuggling ship. Pick up cargo, sail it from island to island and deliver it without getting sunk.

- **Smuggling runs:** deliver cargo between islands to earn money.
- **Enemy ships:** enemies spawn along your route and attack you.
- **Upgrades:** spend your earnings on more damage, more health or repairs for your ship.

This creates a simple gameplay loop: deliver, earn, upgrade and head back out stronger.
